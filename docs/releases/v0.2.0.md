Series are found far more reliably and can be culled frame by frame in a new review screen. Settings are now split into sections, with an Advanced tab for tuning culling to the way you shoot. RAW views also stay quick when you move through photos quickly.

## What's new

**Series review**
- `Enter` or `R` on a stack opens its series as a review screen: the frames side by side, as large as the screen allows. Each frame shows its sharpness and problems, and the best frame is marked with the reason it was chosen. Clicking a stack's frame count, or *Review N series* in the culling bar, opens it too.
- `←`/`→` go through the frames, `↑`/`↓` go to the series before and after, `Enter` opens a frame in the single view, and `Esc` goes back to the library on that series. In the single view, the arrows and the filmstrip stay within the series.
- **Keep best** (`Shift+K`) keeps the best frame, moves the rest to `_Rejected` as one undoable move, and goes on to the next series. You can also keep or reject a frame from its tile, or keep several selected frames together.
- A series culled down to one frame moves on by itself. Rejecting a series' first frame no longer folds the stack.

**Better series**
- Frames up to 2 seconds apart stay together unless the scene clearly changes. After a longer pause (up to a minute), frames stay in the series only if they look alike, and the longer the pause, the more alike they must be. On a hand-labelled shoot of 669 RAW files, 98% of the frames that belong together are now joined (was 67%), and no two scenes are mixed.

**Settings in sections**
- Settings is split into General, Culling, Viewer, Offline, Advanced and Library, shown as tabs along the top on phones.
- **Advanced** tunes the numbers behind culling for your shooting:
  - how series are cut, with a live preview of how many series your changes make of the open folder or the library
  - how the best frame is chosen
  - when a photo counts as blurry, blinking, clipped or too dark
  - how close Find similar and search by content must be
  - the Duplicates report's levels

  Changes are a draft until you press Apply, each setting can be reset, and the values are kept per library.

**Filters**
- A new filter panel always fits the window (a bottom sheet on phones), with labelled sections. Active filters show as chips in the toolbar.
- The culling bar is one row that scrolls instead of wrapping, with a *Review series* button.

**Faster RAW viewing (FlashRaw 0.4)**
- When you move past a photo before its RAW view is ready, Hyperfocal now stops developing it within a few milliseconds, so the photo you land on gets the whole processor. In tests, that photo's RAW view appeared as fast as on an idle server, where before it took 20–55% longer.
- Culling's background measurements step aside the same way: one in progress stops as soon as you open a RAW view, and that photo is measured again later.
- A photo's RAW render, histogram and clipping mask share one development, which carries on while any of them is still needed.

## Fixes

- Settings and the log showed the wrong FlashRaw version.
- Requests for something that doesn't exist (a folder, album, person or tile), or that can't be carried out, now get a clear error instead of looking like a server failure.

## Install

The image is `ghcr.io/au12/hyperfocal:0.2.0` (linux/amd64). `:0.2` follows the 0.2.x releases, and `:latest` follows `main`. The versioned tags are published once this release's checks pass: the full test suite and the 300,000-photo library test.

- **Unraid:** update the container, or save [`unraid/hyperfocal.xml`](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.2.0/unraid/hyperfocal.xml) as a user template. See [Install on Unraid](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.2.0/README.md#install-on-unraid).
- **Docker Compose:** see the [example](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.2.0/README.md#docker-compose).
