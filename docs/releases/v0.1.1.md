The side panels can now be resized, hidden and arranged, Locations moves into the sidebar with a map that no longer covers everything else, and holding an arrow key in the single view keeps up with you.

## What's new

**Side panels**
- Drag the edge between the sidebar or the info panel and the photos to resize it, or double-click it for the default width. The tab in the middle of the edge hides the panel, and brings it back from the edge of the window.
- Right-click any section or entry of the sidebar or the info panel (hold it on a phone) to move it up or down or hide it. The same menu lists what is hidden, to show it again, and can reset the panel. Albums get their own actions there too.
- *Library tools* (Keywords, Geotag from a track, Duplicates, Archive integrity) is now a section of the sidebar; the wrench menu in the header is gone.

**Locations**
- *Locations* in the sidebar (or `M`) shows every photo with a location on a map, and the search field and filters narrow it. The Grid/Map switch is gone: `M`, `G` or `Esc` goes back to the photos you were looking at.
- The map no longer draws over the single view, menus, dialogs or the phone menu, has no empty band above the world, merges markers that would overlap, and follows the sidebar's width.

**Viewing**
- Holding an arrow key in the single view shows every photo you pass (from its thumbnail), and the full image loads as soon as you let go: a fraction of a second, where a test with 20 MP JPEGs took over 10 seconds before.
- Full screen stays on when you press `Esc` (Chromium browsers), so `Esc` keeps its usual meaning. Leave with the button, `F`, or `Esc` twice quickly on the library page.
- Double-click the EV readout to reset the exposure correction.

**People & pets**
- Cleaner People view, person sheet and info panel rows, with a banner when suggestions are waiting for a yes or no.
- Every face or animal has a menu (⋯ or right-click) to correct the detectors: *Not a face*, *It's a dog*, *It's a cat*.

**AI engine**
- The engine stops when it has nothing to do and gives its memory back, then starts again for the next piece of work. On a GPU it stops after 15 minutes without work, or 2 minutes when no browser is open (`HYPERFOCAL_ML_IDLE_MIN`, `HYPERFOCAL_ML_IDLE_NO_CLIENTS_MIN`); on the CPU it stays up unless you set them.

## Changed shortcuts

- `L` is now linear mode, and `Shift+L` sets the location (they were the other way round). Shortcuts you changed yourself are kept.

## Install

The image is `ghcr.io/au12/hyperfocal:0.1.1` (linux/amd64). `:0.1` follows the 0.1.x releases, and `:latest` follows `main`. The versioned tags are published once this release's checks pass: the full test suite and the 300,000-photo library test.

- **Unraid:** update the container, or save [`unraid/hyperfocal.xml`](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.1.1/unraid/hyperfocal.xml) as a user template. See [Install on Unraid](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.1.1/README.md#install-on-unraid).
- **Docker Compose:** see the [example](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.1.1/README.md#docker-compose).
