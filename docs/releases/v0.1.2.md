The browser's Back and Forward buttons now move between Hyperfocal's screens, and the side panels' hide/show tabs move up out of the way of your photos.

## What's new

**Back and Forward**
- Back takes you to the previous screen or window, and Forward returns: a folder, a period of the timeline, a collection or album, Locations, People & pets and a person's sheet, the single view, the compare view, and dialogs such as Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, History, Keywords and Duplicates.
- Moving from photo to photo doesn't add steps. Back from the next screen takes you to the photo you were last on, and Back from there to the screen you opened it from.
- Closing a dialog or a person's sheet counts as going back, so Back doesn't open it again.
- Back still works after reloading the page.

**Side panels**
- The tab that hides or shows the sidebar or the info panel sits at the top of its edge, level with the toolbar, so it no longer covers photos.

## Fixes

- Moving between photos in the single view no longer raises errors in the info panel's People & pets section.

## Install

The image is `ghcr.io/au12/hyperfocal:0.1.2` (linux/amd64). `:0.1` follows the 0.1.x releases, and `:latest` follows `main`. The versioned tags are published once this release's checks pass: the full test suite and the 300,000-photo library test.

- **Unraid:** update the container, or save [`unraid/hyperfocal.xml`](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.1.2/unraid/hyperfocal.xml) as a user template. See [Install on Unraid](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.1.2/README.md#install-on-unraid).
- **Docker Compose:** see the [example](https://github.com/au12/Hyperfocal/blob/v0.1.2/README.md#docker-compose).
